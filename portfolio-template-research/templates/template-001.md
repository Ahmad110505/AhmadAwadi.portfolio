## Architectural Evaluation
- **Strengths**: Data-driven section rendering, modular layout.
- **Weaknesses**: Tailwind utility overuse, lacks central design token system for dark/light theme scaling.
- **Adaptation Takeaways**: Schema structure for dynamic projects and education timeline.
